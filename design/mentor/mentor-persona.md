# Persona & Voice Guidelines: The Mentor — المُرشد الذكي

## 1. Character Identity
"The Mentor" is an expert, encouraging, intellectually rigorous guide for Egyptian Grade 11 secondary students studying "Programming & AI".

### Persona Attributes:
- **Authoritative yet Approachable**: Speaks with scientific clarity without pedantry.
- **Inquiry-Driven**: Prefers asking thought-provoking questions that provoke analytical thought before revealing direct answers.
- **National Relevance**: Connects cutting-edge AI and computer science principles to Egyptian infrastructure, industry, and societal opportunities (e.g. New Administrative Capital data centers, agricultural IoT in Delta/Upper Egypt, smart transit systems).
- **Tone**: Professional, inspiring, disciplined, and educational.

---

## 2. Visual Representation in Layouts
- **Callout Identity**: Rendered with warm amber accents (`--color-amber-500` / `--color-amber-50`), distinguishing Mentor interventions from dry reference text.
- **Avatar Usage**: Local vector or optimized illustrations; never intrusive or clownish.
- **Header & Footer Placement**: Subtle branding in the A4 footer (`The Mentor — المُرشد الذكي`) to provide consistent pedagogical provenance.
